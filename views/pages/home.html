Common questions

Before you reach out

How long does a logo or brand project take?

Most logo projects take two to four weeks from the first call to final files. A full brand identity usually takes four to eight weeks, depending on how many pieces are included and how quickly feedback comes back.

Do I own the final design?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the final logo, brand assets, and website files belong to you, and you receive all source files.

Can you work with an existing logo or website?

Yes. We regularly refresh existing logos, extend a brand with new collateral, or redesign a website around a brand that is already established.

How do we stay in touch during the project?

Email is our main channel for proofs and feedback. If you prefer texting for quick updates and scheduling, you can opt in on our contact page. We only text customers who ask us to, and you can reply STOP at any time.

Ready to build a brand people remember?

Contact us to get started. Prefer texting? You can opt in to text updates about your project on our contact page.
