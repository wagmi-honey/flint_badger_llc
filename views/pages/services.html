01 · Logo design

A logo that works everywhere

We design original, professional logos built to work at every size, from a business card to a storefront sign. Each project starts with a conversation about your business and audience, moves through a set of concepts and revisions, and ends with a final logo delivered in every file format you need for print, web, and social media.

Good for: new businesses, rebrands, and established companies whose logo no longer fits who they are.

What's included

  • Discovery call and written creative brief
  • Two to three initial logo concepts
  • Two rounds of refinements
  • Primary logo, secondary mark, and icon
  • Full color, one color, and reversed versions
  • SVG, PDF, PNG, and JPG files, organized and labeled
  • A one-page guide to using your logo correctly

03 · Custom website design

A website built around your customers

We design and build modern, mobile-friendly websites around your brand and your customers. Every site is fast, easy to navigate, and simple for you to update, with clear pages that explain what you do and make it easy for visitors to get in touch.

Good for: service businesses, shops, restaurants, and professionals who need a site that is clear, fast, and easy to maintain.

Process

What working with us looks like

  1. Discovery call

    A 30-minute conversation about your business, customers, and what you need. You'll have a written quote and timeline within two business days.

  2. Research and brief

    We look at your market and competitors and write a short creative brief that we both sign off on before design begins.

  3. Concepts

    You see a small set of directions with a short explanation of each. We walk through them together and pick one to develop.

  4. Refinement

    Two rounds of changes are built into every project. Most projects need fewer.

  5. Delivery and handoff

    Final files, guidelines, or a live website, plus a short walkthrough so you know exactly how to use everything.

FAQ

Questions we hear most

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted individually based on scope. After a short discovery call you'll receive a fixed written quote, so there are no surprises later. Contact us with a few details and we'll get back to you within one business day.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of your business, who your customers are, and any examples of brands or websites you like. If you have an existing logo, photos, or copy, send those too. We'll guide you through the rest on the discovery call.

Can you print business cards or signage?

We design the files and prepare them to your printer's specifications. We can also recommend printers we've worked with and coordinate with them on your behalf.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. Every website project ends with a walkthrough showing you how to change text, images, and hours. If you'd rather we handle updates, we can arrange that too.

Do you work with businesses outside Kentucky?

Yes. Most of our work happens over email, video calls, and shared proofs, so location isn't a limitation. We work with businesses across the United States.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you need and we will reply within one business day.
